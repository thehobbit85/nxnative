# NVN replay parity with the Apple renderer

The contract is the observable behavior of the public NVN calls on the Mac and iOS. A record may cover multiple entry points and payload forms. Games supply comparative benchmarks and integration smoke tests. The cases below use synthetic inputs. See the [host and builder plan](android-parity.md) for end-to-end qualification.

**Compared revisions.** The active branch is `codex/cross-platform-runtime`, rebased onto Apple upstream `777a5f6c618a38cd2d7f795f465466fb56920534`, with Android integration at `b96b887f`. The original inventory used Vulkan `4a93a069074bfc4da3fa7394af82dd6fa8db4eac` and Apple `de23ae82ad35453417ed4eda942d27d07e5d74df`; the source line references below remain historical locators from that inventory. Current conformance claims are the evidence text, not those old line numbers. Upstream additions, including shared sparse physical tiles, are parity obligations.

**Source map.** R is `dm/gfx/nvn/hle_nvn.c` (command writer at 829–1166, 2360–2369, 2532–2584 and 2689–2738). H is `dm/gfx/nvn/nvn_obj.h` (enum and payloads, 100–195). M is `dm/gfx/nvn/nvn_mtl.m` (main execution switch 11143–11276, state 7843–7905, draw 9830–10250 and transfer/compute 10251–10939). V is `dm/gfx/nvn/vulkan/nvn_vk.c` at the committed revision (dispatch switch 1662–1722). F is the committed `dm/gfx/nvn/vulkan/tests/t_replay.c` (synthetic lists, 18–123). References in the table identify the consuming code as well as the record.

**Evidence.** “Fixture-proven slice” means a checked outcome in F depends on this narrow path in the committed, driver-backed test suite. The full and compact draws and the pitched two-layer readback passed on ARM64 Android 15 with llvmpipe. “Partial” means a known missing or divergent behavior, even when F exercises a subset. “Handler unverified” means a plausible consumer exists but F supplies no distinguishing oracle. “Missing” means the committed replay cannot deliver the stated reference feature. After rebasing onto upstream main at `1d4b8e82`, the 43 explicit Vulkan cases and 12 default cases account for 55 active records. Upstream added `OP_MULTI_DRAW` for large indirect draw lists; its Vulkan implementation remains open. `OP_BEGIN` is obsolete and `OP_COUNT_` is the sentinel.

**Transfer conformance.** The public NVN fixture allocates a guest memory pool, creates the texture and view, records layered clears and a pitched subregion copy, submits the resulting bytes, then waits through public FenceSync/SyncWait. It verifies every destination byte including the guard regions and row/image padding. The same fixture runs on Metal and Vulkan. On Apple Silicon with a Vulkan SDK and driver installed, run `make -C dm/gfx/nvn/vulkan GAME=<profile-id> recorded-transfer-conformance`. Use a profile from the repository. The NDK `android-recorder` target builds the same public fixture for ARM64 Android. Run `t_recorded_transfer_android` on a device with Vulkan. No game data is needed. Compressed synthetic capture files also round-trip in both directions between Apple libcompression and the reference LZFSE implementation on Android. The other synthetic draw and compute cases currently submit records directly. Independent same-list GPU-consumer and 3D transfer cases are still needed.

## Work outside the command stream

- **Typed buffer descriptors.** F:typed_buffers covers sampled/standalone/storage texel views, float/signed/unsigned types, arrays and missing sampled elements, same-size format reinterpretation, size queries, partially overlapping SSBO/image atomics and ordered GPU ownership. `typed_graphics` additionally covers shared vertex/fragment atomics, sampled handles produced on the GPU, three ordered draws and a following compute reader across submission modes. Aligned views share submission backing and survive through its fence.
